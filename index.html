Research summary & evidence

With the same leaderboard data, model ranks differed by up to 77 positions across aggregation methods. I studied how much we can trust rankings when scores are selectively missing.

A comparison of mean-score and latent-trait ranks for 162 models. This is a new visualization of the original analysis data, not evidence of improved accuracy or true model superiority.
